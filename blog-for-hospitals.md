# Why Hospitals Are Bringing Their Front Desk Onto Welleni

If your hospital's appointment booking still runs through a landline, a diary, and whoever happens to pick up the phone, you already know where the friction is: missed calls become missed patients, and every no-show is a slot you can't get back.

Welleni gives your hospital a direct digital front door — without replacing the systems you already run internally.

## Get discovered by patients actually near you

When your hospital registers on Welleni, you're listed to patients searching nearby — sorted by real distance, not a paid placement. No SEO games, no directory fees. Just visibility to the people who are geographically closest and most likely to actually show up.

## Bookings land in your dashboard, not a diary

Every patient who books through Welleni is tied directly to your hospital account. Log into your dashboard and see upcoming appointments — who's coming, for what, and when — without a single phone call.

## Payment confirmed before the patient walks in

Appointments are paid for upfront through Razorpay, with the transaction verified on our servers before it's ever marked complete. That means no disputes at the front desk about whether payment went through — it's already settled.

## Your team gets notified too

The moment a patient books and pays, your hospital gets a WhatsApp message with the patient's name, contact number, and appointment details — so your staff knows what's coming in, even before the patient arrives.

## Set up once, not per booking

Registration takes a few minutes: your hospital details, registration number, and location. From there, every new patient search that matches your area surfaces you automatically — no manual listing updates required.
