# What's Actually Happening Behind Every Welleni Booking

Healthcare software has to earn trust differently than other apps — a bug in a shopping cart is annoying, a bug in a booking system can mean someone misses care they need. Here's what's actually built into Welleni to make sure that doesn't happen.

## Every account starts with mobile verification

Whether you're a patient or a hospital, signing up requires verifying your mobile number with a one-time code before the account goes live. It's a small step that closes off a lot of the fake-account and spam problems booking platforms tend to have.

## Payments are verified, not assumed

A lot of booking apps trust whatever the payment provider's popup tells them. Welleni doesn't — every payment is independently verified against Razorpay's own records on our servers before an appointment is ever marked as paid. If it isn't genuinely confirmed, it isn't confirmed.

## Confirmations reach you where you already are

We send booking confirmations straight to WhatsApp — for the patient booking the appointment, and for the hospital receiving it — so nobody has to remember to check an app or an inbox to know a booking went through.

## Built with India's data protection law in mind

We're actively working through compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023 — including a clear privacy notice, purpose-specific consent choices at signup, a way to request your data or ask us to delete it, and a named grievance contact if something needs to be raised. Healthcare data deserves that level of care, and we're building toward it deliberately rather than treating it as an afterthought.

## Built for Hyderabad, growing from here

Welleni started with a simple question: why does booking a hospital appointment in Hyderabad still feel harder than it needs to be? Everything we've built — location-based hospital search, real-time slot booking, verified payments, instant confirmations — comes back to answering that, for patients and hospitals alike.
