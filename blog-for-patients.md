# Booking a Hospital Appointment Shouldn't Feel Like a Second Job

Anyone who has tried to book a hospital appointment in Hyderabad knows the drill: call the front desk, get put on hold, call back, hope someone remembers to confirm, then still show up unsure if the slot is real. Welleni exists because that shouldn't be the norm anymore.

## Find a hospital near you, not just any hospital

Welleni shows you real, registered partner hospitals sorted by distance from where you are — not a generic directory listing. Open the app, tap "Book Appointment," and you'll see hospitals closest to you first, with their location and contact details right there.

## Pick your own time slot

No more guessing whether a hospital is free. Choose a date up to a week out, then pick from actual available time slots — morning or afternoon — and know exactly when you're expected.

## Pay securely, get confirmed instantly

Payments go through Razorpay with full signature verification on our end, so there's no ambiguity about whether your payment went through. The moment it's confirmed, your appointment is locked in — no follow-up call needed.

## Get your confirmation on WhatsApp — instantly

As soon as your appointment is booked, a confirmation lands in your WhatsApp with your doctor's name, hospital, date, time, and payment receipt — all in one message you can screenshot and show at the front desk.

## Your data, handled properly

Every account on Welleni goes through mobile OTP verification before it's activated, and we're actively building out compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act — including clear consent choices, a data rights request process, and a dedicated grievance channel, so you always know what's collected and why.
